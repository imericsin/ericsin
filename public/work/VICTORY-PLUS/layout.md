## The Challenge

Victory+ partners directly with sports franchises to give local fans access to their teams outside legacy cable distribution.

I inherited a broken design system and fragmented team, and repositioned design from downstream execution to upstream strategy.

The inherited product carried a decade of debt: an unusable, gigabyte-sized design system, no process, and leadership without the taste to catch bad work before it shipped. Teams lacked direction, so work expanded to fill whatever time was available.

::03_FULL

::04_TEXT
## Redesigning the Product

The -1 to 0 to 1 framework describes the journey: -1 was ripping out inherited tech debt and broken design decisions; 0 was establishing a foundation; 1 is the competitive modern product today.

I started by consulting with leads across Android, iOS, and web to understand each platform's constraints and native capabilities, then built a layered system: primitives (colors, typography) that stayed constant across platforms, and semantic components that adapted to each platform's affordances.

The new design system ultimately moved us towards more accurate implementation and development while moving us steadily towards experience parity across competitor services.

::05_FULL

::07_TEXT
## Constraint as a Design Strategy

Constrained resources forced a more disciplined approach — rather than spreading effort evenly, we invested where it mattered most: structure & direct guidance for Android, autonomy for iOS within HIG, and web running on tokens mapped straight to Tailwind CSS.

In addition, I introduced a phased process—Concept → Design → Build —that forced alignment upfront instead of endless revisions; the result was work shipping with significantly less rounds of feedback instead of immeasurable iterations.

## Brand Design Informed by Product

The formerly loud branding was rebuilt using product constraints as the baseline. Color moved out of the product and into team-driven marketing, while brand identity shifted its focus of expression onto typography, a refined logo, and graphic systems drawn from real stadium and court geometry.

In addition, we had to rework the core logotype in a way that would be more of an optimization than an overhaul. VICTORY+ already had built equity amongst a standing audience of 200K+ active users. Any adjustments had to be made in consideration of existing brand equity—forcing restrained, deliberate adjustments.

## The Vision

Design is now helping shape a new vision—reimagining the experience as more creator-led and community-driven, closer in spirit to Twitch than Netflix.

Teams remain the entry point, but co-streaming, chat, and creator communities are what keep fans coming back.

As of 2026, we've begun exploring MCP and LLM-driven workflows to bring designers closer to code, building narrative-driven, high-fidelity concept prototypes that push storytelling further than static mockups ever could—and using them to help shape the roadmap.

## Learnings & Reflection

Success showed up in trust, not dashboards: design became a strategic partner, and partnerships grew sixfold in one year.

The lesson—design leadership means believing design matters enough that every decision ties back to it, with humility and a willingness to be wrong.
