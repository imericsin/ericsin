## Overview

LilyLink is building the first care platform purpose-built for gestational diabetes — a condition affecting 1 in 7 pregnancies, yet still managed largely through paper logs and apps designed for someone else.

I partnered with founder Tyler Rice to sharpen the company's brand as it moved from idea to market — starting with UX for web and iOS, then expanding into a series of workshops to establish the visual and narrative foundation the product deserved.
