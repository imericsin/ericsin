## Overview

The Lime website modernization was a favorite project—a focused sprint creating a sophisticated web presence. I worked with one of my favourite designers in Ray Sison (Playlist.Studio) to design a new look & feel for their marketing site.

I put together a versatile component library for rapid page creation and prototyped its usage with Webflow for better dev handoff, along with providing concepts for "Rider stories" — a community driven outlet to strengthen user connections and anchor strategic brand expression.
